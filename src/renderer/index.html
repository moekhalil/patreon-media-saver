Patreon Media Saver

Sign in to Patreon to see your memberships. A normal Patreon login window opens — enter your email/password and the code Patreon emails you. Your session is stored locally in this app only.

Alternative: paste cookies from your browser

In your browser DevTools → Network, right-click any patreon.com/api/… request → Copy → Copy as fetch, and paste just the value of the "cookie" header (must contain session_id=…).
